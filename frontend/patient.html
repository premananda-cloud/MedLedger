patient guide

You own your data. No one accesses your records without permission.

Getting started

Register

  • Open registration page. Enter username, email, password.
  • System generates a cryptographic key pair.
  • Download and store your private key safely. This is the only key that unlocks your data. Do not share it.
  • Confirm your email via verification message.
⚠️ Your private key is never sent to the server. Only you have access to it.

Log in

  • Open login page. Enter username and password.
  • You are redirected to your dashboard.

Managing your records

Upload a medical record

  • Go to dashboard → "Upload Record".
  • Select a file (PDF, image, or text). Add a description.
  • Click "Upload". The record is encrypted before storage – only you control the key.

View your records

  • "My Records" shows all uploaded records.
  • Click a record to see details, check who has access, download your copy.

Grant access to a doctor

  • "Grant Access" → select doctor by ID/name → pick the record.
  • Set a time window (e.g., 2 hours). After expiry, access is automatically denied.
  • Choose permission level: "View Only" or "View and Download".
  • Click "Grant Access with Signature". The system signs the permission using your private key – this proves you authorized the access.

Revoke access

  • "Active Permissions" → find the permission → "Revoke Access".
  • Access is denied immediately. The action is recorded in the audit trail.

Audit trail

  • "Audit Trail" shows all access attempts: successful, failed, grants, revocations.
  • Click any entry to see who, when, and the result. The audit trail cannot be deleted or altered.

Recover a lost private key

  • "Security" → "Recover Access".
  • Verify identity via email and security questions.
  • Request approval from three trustees.
  • System reconstructs your key – you receive a temporary key (valid 72 hours).
  • Set a new permanent key.
